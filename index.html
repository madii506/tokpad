From a handle to the For You page

Four steps. One public paper trail.

Nothing here needs the creator to do anything. They post like normal and their next video gets more views than it should.

01

Pick a creator

Any handle on TikTok. We read the account live — real avatar, real followers, real video count — before anything is signed.

02

One transaction

The coin's description carries one line, promotes @handle via TokPad. That line is the pairing. It cannot be edited after launch.

03

The push

Every time the fee covers one promotion at TikTok's rate, their newest video is promoted onto For You. One push per hour at most.

04

The receipt

Every push is posted with TikTok's own Promote receipt, the spend, and the views before and after. Not a card we drew.

No dashboard, no trust me

Proof of push.

A promotion is a public event on TikTok — the views are on the video for anyone to see. You do not need us to confirm it, you need the link.

Open the video

Every push on the wall links the exact video. Open it. The view count is on it; TikTok shows it, not us.

Match the time

The wall shows the minute the promotion was bought. TikTok's Promote receipt carries its own timestamp. They agree to the minute, or the wall is wrong and you caught it.

Match the pairing

The coin's description names the creator. The video belongs to that creator. If those two ever disagree, TokPad is lying and the proof is public.

Straight answers

FAQ

Do I need the creator's permission?

No. Promotion is something anyone can buy for any public video through TikTok's ad tools. The coin is about them; the views land on their video; they can ignore all of it. Nothing on this site implies they endorse anything.

Why views instead of money?

Because on TikTok, views are the money. Every other pad sends creators cash they have to go and claim. A promotion needs nothing from them and turns into followers, brand deals and live gifts on its own. They can ignore a tip. Nobody ignores the For You page.

Which video gets promoted?

Their newest public video at the moment of each push. If they haven't posted since the last push, the fund waits, published, until they do. It is never moved to a different creator.

How many views does a push buy?

TikTok decides, per video, per audience. We don't estimate it and the calculator doesn't pretend to. The receipt shows the real views before and after, which is the only honest number.

Where do the numbers on this page come from?

Followers, likes, video counts and avatars are read live from TikTok's own page payload. The calculator's defaults are typed in by us and labelled; change them. There are no measured numbers on this site yet because no coin exists yet.

Are the creators on this page involved?

No. They are examples of the kind of account you could pair. None were contacted, none endorse anything, none have received anything, and no coin has been launched on any of them.
